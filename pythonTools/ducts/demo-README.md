# HVAC demo data (synthetic)

Generated by `pythonTools/ducts/make_demo_data.py` (fixed seed: same output every run). Do not edit by hand.
Whole pipeline in one command (generate, Export-DuctsViewer -Run -Revit, Run-SapDucts with steel and supports):

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File <repo>\scripts\duct\Run-Demo.ps1 -Root <this folder> [-NoSap]

Outputs: `out\revit.*` (overlay, `revit_clean.bin` = ducts + steel + supports, frames / steel / supports CSVs,
match.txt) and `sap\demo.sdb`. Data only: `python <repo>\pythonTools\ducts\make_demo_data.py --out <folder>`.
Layout mirrors the production work folder.

**Known transform:** plant = Rz(30 deg) * revit + (812.5, 431.25, 92.0) ft. The overlay fits rot 30.006 deg with a 0.01 ft median. The bend centres differ between the two sources, as in real data.

## Plant layout (feet)
Rooms by X: ROOM-01 is 1000 to 1060, ROOM-02 is 1060 to 1120, ROOM-03 is 1120 to 1180. Some SYSB rows carry room `ROOM-02A` (the room-variant rule). Floor is EL 100, ceiling EL 130, walls at Y 500 and Y 540.

System names are `SYSA` / `SYSB` / `SYSC`, not `SYS-A`: RunName is `A-B1-<service>-DUCT-<n>`, and the service field cannot contain a hyphen.

## Steel (steel.csv, RunName RACK-1)
- Columns are W10X49 (centred, CP blank), on lines X 1000..1180 every 20 ft at Y 510 / 530, from EL 100 (open vertical, so fixed) up to EL 115.
- Longitudinal beams are W14X30, CP 8, top EL 115. Collinear splices: Y 510 at X 1045 / 1100 / 1145; Y 530 at 1045 / 1060 / 1100 / 1145.
- Cross beams are W12X26, CP 8, at every column line and at mid-bay. The mid-bays at X 1090 and 1150 are left out, which gives 20 ft unsupported duct spans.
- 4-way node at (1060, 530, 115): the two longitudinal ends, a cross beam end and a cantilever meet the column top.
- Cantilevers into the walls: (1060, 530) to Y 540 and (1140, 510) to Y 500. These are open horizontal ends, so pinned.
- Braces are W8X31, sloped (56 deg), in the end bays.
- Hung frames from the ceiling at X 1114, 1122, 1130, 1140, 1150, 1160, 1170: W6X15 hangers from EL 130 (open vertical, so fixed) and a W10X33 beam (CP 8, top EL 122) at Y 521.5..529.5. Hangers and beam meet at corners.
- A stray W6X15 at X 1186..1192, EL 121 is far from any other steel (OPEN_FAR).
- **Duct gaps** (duct bottom to beam top): SYSA 0.5 in, SYSB 2.0 in (rack) and 1.5 in (hung), SYSC 4.0 in, 18x36 4 in. The branches, the fragment and EXH sit off the steel.
