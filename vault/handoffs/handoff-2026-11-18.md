> [!info]- SAP behaviours worth knowing
> All are in [[vault/format/sap-s2k-tables|sap-s2k-tables]]:
> - A ground displacement through a `Fixed` link DOF reaches the next joints as unbalanced loads. Use a stiffness.
> - Frames auto-mesh at any joint on their span by default.
> - A link shorter than the merge tolerance (0.1 in) is zero-length, with local 1 = +Z.
> - Friction isolators carry no friction before they are loaded. Start thermal cases from the weight case.
