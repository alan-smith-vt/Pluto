# Pluto 2026-11-24b: a STAAD frame's results in the viewer; a model made in STAAD to the workbook and back

Cloud session on `pipestress`, the one of [[vault/handoffs/handoff-2026-11-23|handoff-2026-11-23]], continued. The
user asked whether the frame `.anl` reader the other session brought in ([[vault/handoffs/handoff-2026-11-24|handoff-2026-11-24]])
works with Pluto's binary and the viewer. It did not: it wrote CSV files only. Then they asked about code checks.

Of the ways offered, the user chose a viewer arm, with code ratios per load case: "the user can provide a load list
for code checks and staad will output results per load combo".

> [!info]- What was built
> - **`scripts/arms/StaadToPluto.cs`** and the runner **`scripts/staad/Run-StaadFrame.ps1`**: a frame `.anl` → `<base>.bin`
>   (v4, beams only, Y up, inches) + `<base>.features.json`. It reads the file through `AnlFrameReader`, refuses what
>   the reader marks as an error and passes its warnings on.
> - **Drawn:**
>   - members with STAAD's local axes, releases and offsets;
>   - sections from SAP's library by name (STAAD's older AMERICAN names tried as AISC's; single angles turned to
>     STAAD's principal axes), else fitted to `PRINT MEMBER PROPERTIES`, PRIS as given;
>   - supports as sidecar items.
> - **Per load case:**
>   - member end forces, as the force inside the member at each end (end A's printed force negated, so tension is
>     positive and both ends read alike);
>   - joint displacements (the deformed shape);
>   - reactions (kind `restraint`, colouring the support symbols);
>   - `Code ratio` and `Code case` (kind `code`).
> - **Code checks.**
>   - A `CHECK CODE` under a one-case `LOAD LIST` goes into that case, beside its forces.
>   - Any other check is an entry of its own: each member's governing ratio and case.
>   - Where one code was checked case by case, one more entry holds each member's largest ratio over those cases.
>   - Sidecar groups of the members that FAIL and of those NOT DESIGNED, per code.
> - **Changed in shared code:**
>   - `AnlFrameMember.PropertyInches` (the unit where a member's property stands, for PRIS numbers);
>   - `SapToPluto.FrameSection` is `internal`, so the arm draws a library shape as the SAP arm does.
> - **`viewer/tests/readback_staad.js`** checks an export through the viewer's own reader. `--probe` checks the
>   probe's printed numbers.
> - **Vault:**
>   - [[vault/arms/staad-frame-to-viewer|staad-frame-to-viewer]] (new);
>   - the Arms map;
>   - [[vault/arms/pipe-and-frame-workflow|pipe-and-frame-workflow]] (diagram and runner table);
>   - [[vault/format/v4-schema|v4-schema]] (kind `code`);
>   - the probes' README.

> [!info]- Verified here
> - **The compile:** the C# 5 compile of the arm, the reader and the SAP arm under mono, with no warnings in them.
> - **The probe's `.anl`:**
>   - with a scratch SAP library holding its shapes: 8 sections from the library, the 2L fitted (the scratch library
>     has no 2L), the PRIS 6 in square;
>   - without a library: all fitted (W10X49 within 0.02 in).
>
>   `readback_staad.js --probe` passes both:
>   - forces end A negated, a fixed-ended beam's end moments the same sign;
>   - reactions and displacements as printed;
>   - check 1's ratios and cases;
>   - the angle turned 45°.
> - **A scratch copy of the probe** with one-case `LOAD LIST`s before four checks put their ratios in cases 101, 102
>   and 301. It made the repeated check of 101 an entry of its own, and gave the largest-over-cases entry.
> - **The runner** parses, and under pwsh 7 it checks its paths and refuses an output inside the repo. Its `Export`
>   call needs .NET Framework (the sidecar's `System.Web.Extensions`), so the end-to-end run is the production
>   machine's.

## Later: a model made in STAAD, to the workbook, to SAP or STAAD

The user converted an older STAAD model with `-In <x>.std -Xlsx` and got 174 refusals. These came from units changing
along the file, groups, materials, loads, the analysis, `LOAD LIST` and the prints. They asked for the whole STAAD file
to come into the workbook, design parameters and prints included, and to go out to SAP or STAAD from there. Then they
asked for load envelopes too (`DEFINE ENVELOPE`, `LOAD LIST ENV`; STAAD's TR.40, which they sent).

> [!info]- What changed
> - **`StaadInput`** (`scripts/lib/readers/ANLReader.cs`):
>   - a later `UNIT`'s numbers are converted to the first's, by what each is;
>   - the commands after the analysis are kept in order (load combinations there are read as usual);
>   - `REPEAT LOAD`, the older `LOAD n <title>`, GEOMETRY / FLOOR / SOLID group kinds, and `DEFINE ENVELOPE` are read.
>
>   The unit stamper takes STAAD's plural words too (`KNS`, `METERS`, `CMS` …), for the `.anl` readers as well.
> - **`StdToFrame`:**
>   - unit changes give a notice, no refusal;
>   - GEOMETRY / FLOOR groups are read as member groups;
>   - a material no member uses is left out, and a TYPE other than STEEL is SAP's Other;
>   - `SELFWEIGHT … LIST` of every member counts as none;
>   - `REPEAT LOAD` is read as patterns times factors;
>   - envelopes become SAP Envelope combinations, a load in two envelopes kept in the last;
>   - the read-back check compares commands and envelopes too.
> - **Carried (`StaadCarry` in `FrameStd.cs`):** two tables of Pluto's own in the model's document, the STAAD commands
>   and the STAAD load numbers (envelopes with their TYPE). They show on the workbook's new sheets, STAAD commands and
>   STAAD loads, and can be edited there:
>   - `-Std` writes the commands after the analysis and numbers its loads and envelopes by them;
>   - `-S2k` leaves them out, with a notice.
> - **`FrameStd`:** SAP Envelope combinations are written as `DEFINE ENVELOPE`.
> - **`AnlFrameReader`:** `DEFINE ENVELOPE` and `LOAD LIST ENV` are read, so a `CHECK CODE` under an envelope places
>   its ratios as under any load list (StaadToPluto).
> - **Vault:**
>   - [[vault/format/frame-to-staad|frame-to-staad]]: "A model made in STAAD, and what it carries";
>   - [[vault/arms/frame-workbook|frame-workbook]]: the two sheets;
>   - [[vault/arms/staad-frame-to-viewer|staad-frame-to-viewer]]: envelopes.

> [!info]- Verified here
> - **A file in the form STAAD's editor saves** read exactly and written back the same: units changing in the model
>   sections, GEOMETRY / FLOOR groups, three materials (two unused), `REPEAT LOAD`, `PERFORM ANALYSIS PRINT`, a load
>   combination after the analysis, `LOAD LIST`, prints, design parameters. Further variants:
>   - **STAAD's own load numbers** (10, 101, 201) are kept, so `LOAD LIST 10 101 201` stays right.
>   - **An analysis under INCHES** gets that `UNIT` among the carried commands.
>   - **Envelopes** read and write back the same, 201 moving to the later envelope.
> - **Through the workbook** the result is identical, and edits on the STAAD sheets reach `-Std`: a renumbered load,
>   an added design parameter, an envelope TYPE.
> - **The SAP document** has no STAAD rows.
> - **The probe's `.std`:** 60 refusals before, 3 now. All three are truss and self-weight cases (frame-to-staad, the
>   new callout).
> - **The probe's `.anl` with envelopes added:** `LOAD LIST ENV` expands to its cases.
> - **The suites are unchanged:**
>   - the SAP / CAESAR models' `-Std` round trips are IDENTICAL;
>   - the STAAD-saved files still read the same;
>   - `readback_staad.js --probe` passes.
