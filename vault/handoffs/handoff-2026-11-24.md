# Pluto 2026-11-24: CalcFlow reviewed, its front door started, the frame .anl reader moved to pipestress

Cloud session on branches `CalcFlow` and `pipestress`. The user asked for `CalcFlow` to sit on the latest `pipestress`, for a few agents to review CalcFlow's process against a sketch of a GUI-like workbook, and then for the first step of the result, with the `.anl` parser work written to `pipestress`.

> [!info]- The merges
> - `pipestress` merged into `CalcFlow` three times as it moved (`3c929df`, `6ab8c0f`, `6dd5e3a`). `CalcFlow` is 0 behind.
